# No Internet for Three Weeks
I think it is safe to say that I am the only PhD student in the world who is about to finish his PhD studies and has no access to the internet!
Incompetence, lack of infrastructure, whatever you want to call it, would not justify the denial of internet access for students living 300 meters away from the University of Salerno!
Denial of a problem is how a tired mind tries to protect itself. Just a note I thought might be worth leaving here.

Here is a technical log of my month-long attempt to get access to the internet as an HPC PhD candidate at the University of Salerno!

## LTE 5G coverage
No signal inside the building. No signal outside the building. No signal six meters above any walls in the surroundings.
I have invested and bought these SIM cards out of pocket, along with data packages for each one:
- TIM
- Vodafone
- WOWWW (Austrian company with EU roaming data)
- Iliad
- WindTre

## The Modems
I have tried with my phone, a Moto G15, which is 4G LTE. 
Asked a dear friend to come over and try to be sure, and still did not manage to get any signal
Also tried a USB LTE dongle (E3372h) with no success.

## Symptoms
Unstable connection, packet loss, sub 1 megabit/s downlink, sub 5kilobyte/s uplink!
Latencies over 1000ms with jitter of 600ms.

## Fiber Internet
Companies that I signed a contract with:
- Vodafone
- Iliad

They wasted 2 weeks of my life asking me to wait for the technician. On three occasions, just 12 hours before the appointment, the person responsible for the FiberCop installations of Fisciano postponed the appointment with no reason.
When I called and also complained in person in Salerno, they told me they couldn't help.
I had to cancel both contracts.
These were contracts for one gigabit unlimited fiber internet.

## FWA (stationary 5G)
As an effort to avoid the problematic person in charge of the FiberCop installations in the area of Fisciano, I decided to sign yet another contract with WindTre, for FWA 300 megabit/s stationary 5G internet.
This does not need a fiber connection, and therefore I had hopes that it would work.
October 20 at 14:00, the technician came and tested the LTE and 5G signals around the house, around the roof and walls, and told me it is not possible to install it as there is no signal. "Sorry, Ciao!" and that was that.
He mentioned that my only two options are the Eolo "Laser" modem or Starlink.

## Eolo
According to their website, it comes with a 12-month commitment and fines if you cancel the contract earlier.
I am a student about to graduate! I cannot commit for a year!

## Starlink
Comes with 12-months promotion that offers the Starlink kit for free with no rent. If you cancel the contract earlier, you have to pay a fine.
If you choose to buy a monthly plan, you have to either rent the kit, which costs about 60 euros a month (total). Your last option is to buy a kit which costs 400 euros in Italy.
Shipping takes three weeks!
Don't let me start on that. Why does it take three weeks? You can deliver faster with donkeys!


# Conclusion
No matter what, one cannot justify this level of incompetence in a small community that hosts a large university!
The internet is the backbone. If I had been told three years ago that one day I would have to vent on my weblog because there is no authority/customer service/competent people in charge, and that I cannot get access to the internet as a PhD student living 300 meters away from the university, I would have laughed it off, considering it a joke. Unfortunately, this is the reality of U of Salerno.
